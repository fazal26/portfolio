Klaro.

A connected-device mobile app monitoring humidor temperature and humidity, with personalised alerts when conditions drift — architected for iOS and Android, with the Bluetooth layer built by hand.

RoleMobile Architect
SectorConnected Device
PlatformsiOS · Android
LinkBLE

Problem

// 01

A humidor holds its contents in a narrow band of temperature and humidity. Drift outside it does damage slowly and invisibly, and by the time the owner notices, the damage is done. A sensor in the box is only useful if the phone in their pocket knows what it is reading.

That makes the hard part the link, not the chart. Bluetooth Low Energy connections drop when a phone sleeps, when the user walks out of range, when the OS reclaims the radio. An app that shows a beautiful graph and silently stops reading is worse than no app, because it is trusted.

The reading is easy. Staying connected, reconnecting quietly, and alerting on drift is the product.

Approach

// 02
  • 01
    One React Native codebase, two platformsArchitected the app so iOS and Android share product logic while the parts that genuinely differ — Bluetooth permissions, background behaviour, store requirements — stay isolated rather than smeared through the UI.
  • 02
    A BLE layer built, not borrowedPairing, sensor reads and reconnection were written as a dedicated layer with explicit state, so the rest of the app consumes readings without knowing anything about radio conditions.
  • 03
    Reconnection as the default caseDropped connections are normal, not exceptional. The layer treats reconnection as routine and recovers quietly, instead of surfacing every blip to the user or going silently dead.
  • 04
    Alerts tied to the owner's thresholdsPersonalised alerting on temperature and humidity drift, so the notification means something to that owner's box rather than firing on a generic range.
  • 05
    Shipped through both storesManaged App Store and Play Store releases end to end, including the review requirements that Bluetooth and background activity attract.

Outcome

// 04

Owners see live temperature and humidity from their own humidor and get told when it drifts, on either platform, from one codebase — with the connection handling itself rather than asking to be managed.
